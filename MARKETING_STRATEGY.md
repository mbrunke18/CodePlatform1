## THE STRATEGIC SHIFT

The original strategy positioned Readiness OS primarily as crisis and risk infrastructure. The platform now demonstrates equal capability across three strategic domains:

- **RISK & RESILIENCE** — ransomware, activist investor, supply chain collapse, regulatory inquiry
- **GROWTH & POSITIONING** — competitor product launch, market entry window capture
- **TRANSFORMATION** — talent exodus, M&A, organizational change

**The updated message:** "The response is ready before the trigger fires" applies equally to threats AND opportunities. A competitive window closes just as fast as a ransomware incident. The Fortune 1000 is just as unprepared to seize a market entry window as it is to handle a 3 AM cyberattack.

**The platform is no longer positioned as a crisis tool. It is Readiness Infrastructure for Strategic Trigger Response — the operating model for every domain.**
