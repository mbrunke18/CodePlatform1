How to Use 6 AI Frameworks 
to Transform One Critical Process.

Pick one critical process. Then run it through these 6 frameworks.

Each one is asking a different question

Here is when to use them:

𝗠𝗶𝗰𝗿𝗼𝘀𝗼𝗳𝘁'𝘀 𝗔𝗜 𝗠𝗮𝘁𝘂𝗿𝗶𝘁𝘆 𝗠𝗼𝗱𝗲𝗹
Focus: evolution over time.
Use when: planning a 6 to 18 month roadmap.
Asks: how does human involvement shrink over time?
Today AI flags issues. 
In 6 months it suggests fixes. In 12 it applies them.

𝗣𝘄𝗖'𝘀 𝗔𝗜 𝗔𝘂𝗴𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 𝗦𝗽𝗲𝗰𝘁𝗿𝘂𝗺
Focus: AI's role at each step.
Use when: different steps need different levels of AI.
Asks: should AI advise, assist, execute, or decide?
Step 1 AI advises. 
Step 2 AI executes. 
Step 3 the human decides.

𝗗𝗲𝗹𝗼𝗶𝘁𝘁𝗲'𝘀 𝗔𝘂𝗴𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗱 𝗜𝗻𝘁𝗲𝗹𝗹𝗶𝗴𝗲𝗻𝗰𝗲 𝗙𝗿𝗮𝗺𝗲𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗸
Focus: the type of AI contribution.
Use when: deciding what AI should actually do.
Asks: automate, augment, or amplify?
Automate data entry. 
Augment risk assessment. 
Amplify team capacity.

𝗚𝗮𝗿𝘁𝗻𝗲𝗿'𝘀 𝗔𝘂𝘁𝗼𝗻𝗼𝗺𝗼𝘂𝘀 𝗦𝘆𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗺𝘀 𝗙𝗿𝗮𝗺𝗲𝘄𝗼𝗿𝗸
Focus: the degree of AI independence.
Use when: setting supervision requirements.
Asks: what can AI do without a human?
Manual approval for high-value items. 
AI handles routine items on its own.

𝗠𝗜𝗧'𝘀 𝗛𝘂𝗺𝗮𝗻-𝗶𝗻-𝘁𝗵𝗲-𝗟𝗼𝗼𝗽
Focus: control and override.
Use when: legal, safety, or quality stakes are high.
Asks: where must a human review or step in?
AI processes 95% of cases. 
Humans handle the edge cases and can override anything.

𝗛𝗕𝗥'𝘀 𝗛𝘂𝗺𝗮𝗻-𝗔𝗜 𝗧𝗲𝗮𝗺𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗠𝗼𝗱𝗲𝗹
Focus: the human-AI relationship.
Use when: redesigning daily workflows.
Asks: is AI a tool, a teammate, or a manager?
AI provides data (tool). AI drafts responses (teammate). AI routes tasks (manager).

𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗲𝘅𝗲𝗿𝗰𝗶𝘀𝗲: take one process tomorrow and run it through all six in order.

→ Map the maturity trajectory (Microsoft)
→ Define roles at each step (PwC)
→ Categorize the work type (Deloitte)
→ Set autonomy boundaries (Gartner)
→ Design the oversight points (MIT)
→ Clarify the relationships (HBR)
