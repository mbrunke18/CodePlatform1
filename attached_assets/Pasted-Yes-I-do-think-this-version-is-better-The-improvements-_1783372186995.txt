### What still holds it back

My biggest observation hasn't changed.

The video still spends too much time explaining **what the platform is** before fully convincing me **why I should care**.

An executive audience isn't looking for another platform. They're thinking:

* "How does this reduce my organizational risk?"
* "How does this make my leadership team better?"
* "What problem disappears if I own this?"

Those questions should be answered emotionally before they're answered functionally.

---

By the time Readiness OS is introduced, viewers should be thinking:

> "Let me understand what this does."
