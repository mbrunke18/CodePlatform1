## 3 Fatal Pitch Traps to Avoid in the Room
 1. **Do Not Get Dragged Into a "Feature War":** If an investor asks, *"Can't ServiceNow just add this as a feature?"* your answer must be immediate and structural: *"No, because ServiceNow is an asynchronous ticketing and workflow router built for post-alignment execution.  2. **Over-Emphasize "Humans in the Loop":** Conservative enterprise investors get terrified of AI taking autonomous action in a crisis. Every time you mention the speed of the system, explicitly remind them: **"The system stages the choice in seconds, but an executive must click authorize."**
 3. **Own the Onboarding Reality:** When they ask how long it takes to build 170 protocols for a new enterprise, confidently state: *"They don't build them from scratch. VaughnMartin ships with standard, out-of-the-box readiness protocols for the most common enterprise threats—from cyber breaches to regulatory inquiries. Onboarding isn't a consulting project; it's a configuration process."*
