this is from my whitepaper the start The Eriksson Theory of Enterprise Coordination & Outcome Realization™ For years, I used AI as the most visible example of a deeper problem. Over time, I realized the framework was never really about AI. AI simply exposed the problem more clearly than previous waves of technology. The real challenge facing enterprises is not AI. It is coordination. For more than two decades organizations have struggled with: Strategy execution Digital transformation Data programs Governance Enterprise architecture Shared services Global platforms Risk management Value realization Each initiative was treated as a separate discipline. In reality, they are manifestations of the same underlying problem: The inability to coordinate outcomes across organizational boundaries. Historically, enterprises compensated through: Management hierarchies Committees Governance forums Escalation paths Manual intervention Individual heroics These mechanisms allowed organizations to function despite fragmented ownership, disconnected decisions, and competing objectives. AI did not create this problem. AI exposed it. Autonomous systems, machine-speed decisions, and continuous execution make it impossible to rely on informal coordination mechanisms that have accumulated over decades. The fundamental question therefore is not: "How do we deploy AI?" It is: "How should an enterprise operate to consistently realize outcomes?" This is the foundation of the Eriksson Framework. The framework is a theory of Enterprise Coordination, Outcome Realization, and Enterprise Control. It seeks to answer: Which outcomes matter? Who owns them? Which decisions drive them? What authority exists? How are trade-offs resolved? How is execution coordinated? How does the enterprise learn and adapt? How do outcomes improve over time? From this perspective: Digital transformation is a coordination problem. Data is a coordination problem. Governance is a coordination problem. AI is a coordination problem. Strategy execution is a coordination problem. The enterprise of the future will not be defined by its technology. It will be defined by its ability to sense, decide, coordinate, execute, learn, and adapt around shared outcomes. AI is one chapter in that story. Enterprise Coordination and Outcome Realization is the larger theory.
