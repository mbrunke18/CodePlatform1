# CORE MESSAGING FRAMEWORK

## The Two Messages That Must Be Clear Everywhere

**Message 1: NFL Methodology (WHY it works)**

```
The NFL executes 60-80 critical decisions in 3 hours because of preparation—playbooks built before the season, roles pre-assigned, decisions triggered by situational awareness. ExecuteIQ brings that methodology to enterprise.
```

**Message 2: Platform, Not Fixed Product (WHAT it is)**

```
166 playbooks to start. Customize them. Build your own. The platform handles execution regardless of what's in the playbook.
```

## Key Phrases to Use Throughout

✅ “166 playbooks to start—customize or build your own”
✅ “The playbooks are the accelerant. The platform is the product.”
✅ “Your playbooks, executed in 12 minutes”
✅ “Out of the box or fully custom—the system coordinates either way”
✅ “Start with proven playbooks. Make them yours.”
✅ “The methodology that powers billion-dollar football programs, now a platform for enterprise”

## Phrases to Avoid

❌ “We build custom playbooks for you” (sounds like consulting)
❌ “We’ll work with your team to develop…” (sounds like services)
❌ “Our consultants will…” (obviously bad)
❌ “166 playbooks” without mentioning customization (sounds rigid)

## Founder Story Section

**Section Headline:**

```

**Body:**

```
