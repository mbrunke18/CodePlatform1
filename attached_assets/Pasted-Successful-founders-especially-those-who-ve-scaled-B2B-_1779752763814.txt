Positive Takes – What Would Excite Them
	•	Founder Pedigree & Authentic Pain Insight: Martin Brunke’s claimed execution experience across Ford, Lockheed Martin, Toyota, etc., lands well. Critical Takes – Where They’d Push Back Hard
	•	“12 Minutes” Claim Needs Brutal Proof: Most would say the headline is memorable but dangerous. They’ve seen bold claims kill credibility when real-world friction (politics, legal sign-off, data sensitivity, executive availability) hits. Expect questions like: “What’s the actual median time in messy environments? Show me the distribution, not the best case.”

	◦	Heavy reliance on Microsoft co-sell/partnerships is logical but naive if over-relied upon. Microsoft partners move at their own pace.

Current version feels strong on execution speed but lighter on the proactive “doesn’t exist yet” magic that creates true defensibility. They’d warn against staying in the “faster incident response” bucket instead of owning “strategic mobilization infrastructure.”
Overall Verdict from This Group
“High-upside bet with classic early-stage enterprise risks — worth watching closely, but not yet a ‘must-invest’ or obvious 10x winner.”
They’d rate the idea 8–9/10 and the current execution/proof 4–6/10. Many would advise:

	•	Use those to build public proof (detailed activation reports, ROI calculators, video demos).

	•	Prepare for a long, relationship-heavy sales motion — this isn’t viral bottoms-up.
