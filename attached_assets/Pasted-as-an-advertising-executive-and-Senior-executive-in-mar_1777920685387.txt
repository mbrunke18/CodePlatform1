Perfect prompt. If I were running this as a senior marketing/advertising exec and optimizing for speed to revenue, I’d do this:

1) Don’t sell “platform.” Sell one urgent outcome.
Fastest path is a wedge, not broad positioning.

Wedge positioning (example)
“From trigger to coordinated response in 12 minutes.”
Target one high-pain use case first (pick one):

Cyber/regulatory incident response
Supply-chain disruption response
Executive crisis communications
2) ICP and buyer focus (for fast close)
Prioritize accounts where urgency is already high.

ICP
1,000+ employees
Regulated or high reputational risk
Recent disruption event in last 12 months
Existing spend on consultants + tooling but fragmented process
Buyer map
Economic buyer: COO / CISO / CFO (depends on wedge)
Champion: VP Ops / Risk / Security / Strategy
Approver: CEO + Board-facing stakeholders
3) Offer architecture to close quickly
Use a 3-step commercial path:

Executive Readiness Audit (2 weeks, paid)
Deliverable: scorecard + gap map + trigger playbook blueprint
30-day Pilot
One scenario, one team, one measurable KPI (time-to-coordinate)
Annual Expansion
Add departments, playbook library, integrations
This avoids long “maybe” cycles and gets paid engagement early.

4) Campaign strategy (specific channels + creative)
A) ABM outbound (primary engine)
100 target accounts, 5 contacts/account
Trigger-based outreach (recent breach, recall, leadership change, regulatory event)
7-touch sequence over 14 days (email + LinkedIn + call + short video)
Email subject examples

“If this hits at 7:43am, who coordinates first?”
“Can your exec team mobilize in 12 minutes?”
“Board asks at noon. Team still aligning?”
B) LinkedIn paid (executive audience)
Target titles: COO, CISO, CRO, CFO, Chief Risk Officer, EVP Ops
Creative concept: “72 hours vs 12 minutes”
CTA: “Run your 12-minute readiness simulation”
C) Live executive briefings (high conversion)
30-minute closed-door virtual sessions by industry
Format: 10 min “failure pattern” + 10 min live simulation + 10 min Q&A
Goal: book audit meetings, not generic demos
5) Messaging that sells
Hero message
“When disruption hits, your response system is your strategy.”

Proof stack on every page/deck
Time-to-coordinate improvement
Stakeholder mobilization speed
Decision latency reduced
Financial exposure reduced / avoided
(Use customer-validated numbers wherever possible.)

6) Must-have sales assets (build immediately)
1-page executive brief (problem, value, outcomes)
8-slide C-suite deck
2-min product narrative video
Role-based demo scripts (CEO, COO, CISO, CFO)
ROI calculator with conservative assumptions
Security/compliance one-pager for procurement
7) 30-day sprint plan (fastest execution)
Week 1
Lock wedge + ICP + message
Build target account list
Finalize landing page + outbound sequence + demo script
Week 2
Launch ABM outbound + LinkedIn
Start executive briefing invites
Week 3
Run first 2 briefings
Convert to paid audits
Week 4
Close first pilot cohorts
Publish first quantified customer outcome

Pilot conversion rate from audits
