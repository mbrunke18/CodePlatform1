# 🌐 Website Review — ExecuteIQ

## 1) First Impression & Positioning

**Headline:**

> “The Execution Infrastructure Enterprises Are Missing.” ([ExecuteIQ][1])

### What works

* Strong, provocative framing — positions the product as a **category creator**, not a tool.
* “Execution Infrastructure” signals platform vs. point solution.
* Speaks directly to enterprise pain (delivery failure, fragmentation, lack of visibility).

### Risks / gaps

* The term **“Execution Infrastructure”** is powerful but abstract.
* First-time visitors may ask:

  * Is this software, consulting, or both?
  * Is it predictive AI, orchestration, or PMO tooling?
* Category creation requires **fast grounding** in something familiar.

**Recommendation**
Add a sub-headline that translates the category:

> “AI-powered execution intelligence platform that predicts delivery risk, orchestrates work, and automates enterprise execution.”
