# I Think the "System of Record" Positioning Can Be Even Stronger

They recommend:

> System of Record for Strategic Readiness

I like it.

But I actually think investors will lean forward more with:

> **The Enterprise System of Record for Strategic Situations.**

Why?

Because readiness is an attribute.

Situations are an entity.

Systems of record are almost always organized around entities:

CRM → Customers

ERP → Resources

HRIS → Employees

PLM → Products

Readiness OS → Strategic Situations

The readiness score becomes one property of that object.

That framing is cleaner from a software architecture standpoint.

# My Recommendation Before You Implement Anything

This is probably the most important advice I can give after everything we've built together.

**Don't implement all of these ideas immediately.**

Instead, separate them into three buckets:

### Implement now (messaging changes)

* Refine homepage positioning around "system of record."
* Emphasize Strategic Situations in product descriptions.
* Clarify the category narrative.

### Design for V2 (product architecture)

* Make Strategic Situation the primary object.
* Elevate per-situation readiness scores.
* Surface the Readiness Ledger.
* Add Executive Decision Intelligence.

### Preserve as the long-term vision

* Enterprise Memory.
* Industry benchmarking.
* Enterprise Readiness Index.
* AI reasoning over organizational history.

The mistake many founders make is trying to build all three buckets at once. The strongest product companies keep the long-term vision stable while making only the next layer visible to customers.
