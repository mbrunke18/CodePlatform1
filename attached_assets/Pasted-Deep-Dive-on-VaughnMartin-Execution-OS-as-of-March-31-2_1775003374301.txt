Weaknesses / Scaling Risks:

Replit is convenient but not enterprise-grade for SOC 2 / data residency at scale.
	•	AI Cost/Latency: Heavy GPT-4o reliance (execution briefs, simulations, recorder). Fallbacks exist, but monitor token spend and consider Azure OpenAI + caching.
	•	Signal Quality: RSS-only for now (NYT, BBC, SEC, etc.). Good start, but pilots will reveal false-positive noise. Future: add internal data feeds (ERP, CRM, news APIs).
	•	No per-seat pricing in Growth tier is bold but limits upside if SMBs want granular control.
Overall: 9/10 for a March 2026 launch. The dev reference is one of the cleanest I’ve seen—obsessive about preventing regressions.
