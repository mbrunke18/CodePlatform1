## How to Set Up LinkedIn Products Tab

1. Go to your **VaughnMartin LinkedIn Company Page**
2. Click **Edit page** → **Products** tab → **+ Add product**
3. For each product (01–10):
   - Upload the matching `product-XX.png` as the product logo
   - Copy-paste the Name, Description, and 3 Highlights from above
   - Set the CTA button label and URL
4. Publish — each product gets its own dedicated page on LinkedIn

**Pro tip:** Pin your top 3 products to the Featured section of the Products tab.
The recommended order: Readiness OS Platform → 12-Minute Execution → Founding Partner Program.
