What the greatest business builders actually did
They did not describe their product. They named a problem the world did not have language for yet and then became the only credible source of that language.
Marc Benioff did not sell CRM software. He declared the end of software and spent years making "no software" the category rallying cry before Salesforce was the obvious answer. The category came first. The product became inevitable.
Reed Hastings did not sell DVD rentals or streaming. He named the enemy as late fees and the constraint as the cable bundle. By the time Netflix was dominant the category he had named made every competitor look like the past.
Steve Jobs did not sell a phone with a better keyboard. He said you are carrying three devices and that is the problem. The iPhone was the answer to a problem he named before he revealed the solution.
Jensen Huang did not sell chips. He spent years insisting that the world was moving to accelerated computing and that general purpose computing was hitting a wall. By the time AI exploded NVIDIA was already the infrastructure of the future in the minds of everyone who mattered.
The pattern across all of them
They named the era first. Then they named the gap the era created. Then they named their product as the only logical response to that gap.
They did not compete with existing solutions. They made existing solutions irrelevant by defining a new category of problem that existing solutions were never designed to solve.
