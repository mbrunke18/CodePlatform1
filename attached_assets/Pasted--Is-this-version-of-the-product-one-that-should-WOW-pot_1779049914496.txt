Without having a customer how can we remove all other issues that still make them feel they must buy

Great question.
Truth is: you can’t make rational buyers “must buy” without any customer proof — but you can remove almost every other reason to say no.
The goal
Make the buying decision feel like:
High downside if they wait + low downside if they start now
What to do immediately (pre-customer “must buy” stack)
1) Sell the cost of inaction, not just product features
Build 3 quantified scenarios (cyber, regulatory, opportunity loss) showing:
delay cost (30-day mobilization)
12-minute response value
expected financial delta
Use public benchmarks + transparent assumptions.
2) Offer a risk-reversal commercial structure
If no customers yet, remove purchase fear:
90-day Founding Partner engagement
clear success criteria at day 30/60/90
fee credit to year-1 contract (or partial refund trigger)
This says: “You’re not betting blindly.”
3) Turn your product into a proof machine
In every demo, run their scenario live:
trigger they pick
roles they pick
approvals they pick
simulation + readiness score in real time
No generic demo. Always custom.
4) Pre-answer procurement/security objections
Have a one-page “enterprise readiness packet” ready:
data handling model
authorization/governance model
auditability/versioning
integration architecture
deployment model and controls
No customer references? Then your trust docs must be excellent.
5) Package an implementation certainty plan
Give a concrete “first 14/30/90 days” path:
week 1: scenario selection + authority mapping
week 2–4: protocol setup + simulation drills
day 45+: live shadow mode
day 90: ROI/business case closeout
Uncertainty kills deals more than price.
6) Build synthetic case studies (explicitly labeled)
Use public incidents and show:
“what happened traditionally”
“what this would have looked like in your platform”
timeline + dollars + governance path
Be transparent that they’re modeled, not customer outcomes.
7) Give the champion a board/CFO-ready memo
One page they can forward internally:
problem
financial exposure
why now
why your approach
what approval is requested today
If they can’t resell internally, they won’t buy.
Fast formula
To create “must buy” pressure without customers:
Urgency (cost of delay) + Safety (risk-reversal) + Credibility (proof artifacts) + Ease (clear 90-day path).
