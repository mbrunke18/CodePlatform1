My Take On AI
I have spent the last two years in rooms with CEOs and leadership teams talking about AI, and I will tell you something that might surprise you. Most of those conversations go in circles. 

Not because the leaders aren’t smart, they are some of the sharpest people I know. They go in circles because the questions they keep asking are the wrong ones. They ask, “What AI tool should we be using?” That’s not the right question. 

The real question is: Where should AI live inside how we run our company? 

Those two questions may sound similar, but they are not. One leads to a pile of disconnected tools, an “innovation tax,” and a team that’s confused about which AI tool to use this week. The other leads to a company that runs better. That distinction is at the heart of why we just launched the Rhythm Systems AI Connector into Beta, and why I want to share my broader perspective on where AI is taking strategy execution. 

Every category of software now has an "AI feature." Most of them are general-purpose assistants on general-purpose data, fast, but blind to where execution actually breaks down inside your company.

That's the part I want to talk about today, because it is the part that matters.

AI without a proven execution framework is just a faster way to ask the wrong questions.
