## The shift I'd recommend

Up to this point, much of your work has understandably centered on **explaining Readiness OS**.

The next phase is different.

Your content should increasingly explain **why the category must exist**, with Readiness OS presented as the leading implementation of that vision.

That's a subtle but important change. Companies that successfully establish new categories often spend as much effort teaching the market a new way to think as they do describing their own product.

If you execute that consistently, your website, videos, keynote presentations, LinkedIn presence, sales conversations, analyst briefings, and partner materials all reinforce the same progression:

1. There is an unrecognized enterprise problem.
2. Existing software categories don't fully solve it.
3. A new operating model addresses that gap.
4. Readiness OS is a mature realization of that operating model.

At that point, your marketing is no longer just generating leads—it is shaping how buyers evaluate the entire space. That's how category leaders begin to emerge.
