Strong page overall. The structure is compelling. The argument flows precisely. Problem with a price tag. Proof. Moat. ROI. Comparison. Decision. That sequencing is exactly right for a buyer who arrives skeptical.

Before you use this page in any external context I want to flag four specific things that need attention. Some are critical.

Critical — Trigger count is wrong throughout the page

The page shows 231 Executive Triggers in the footer metrics and in the Founding Partner row of the ROI section. The canonical locked number is 221. This appears multiple times and needs to be corrected before this page is shared with any prospect investor or partner.

Critical — The proof metrics imply external customer validation that does not yet exist

The proof section states zero in regulatory penalties in documented activations using pre-staged disclosure protocols. And the ADVANCE section shows specific metrics. Average 2.3 updates per activation. Typical success rate 74 percent. Average 4.2 minutes saved per update. Protocol library coverage after 12 months 68 percent.

Those metrics are presented as documented outcomes. A sophisticated buyer reading this page will ask where those numbers come from and who the customers are that produced them. If the answer is internal platform testing rather than external customer deployments that needs to be stated clearly or the framing needs to change.

I want to be straight with you about this because it matters commercially and ethically. The page as written implies proven external customer results. The honest framing would be projected based on platform architecture and internal validation or benchmark based on industry data. Not documented activations without that qualification.

Moderate — Fortune 1000 in the meta tags

The meta description reads Fortune 1000 enterprises take 30 days to mobilize. That is the retired exclusive qualifier. Should be startup to Fortune 500 or simply enterprises take 30 days to mobilize.

Minor — Footer tagline

The canonical tagline is the response is ready before the trigger fires. The footer already has that. The we redesign how work flows line creates a competing message.
