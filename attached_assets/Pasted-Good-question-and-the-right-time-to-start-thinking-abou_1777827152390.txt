What the Founding Partner conversation actually looks like
When someone agrees to a 20 minute call you need a clear agenda for what that conversation produces.
First five minutes. Let them tell you what their current mobilization challenge looks like. Do not pitch. Listen. Ask one specific question about the last time they faced a strategic trigger and what the response looked like.
Next ten minutes. Walk them through one of the canonical demo scenarios that matches their industry. Use the Colonial Pipeline or Change Healthcare scenario if they are in a security-adjacent role. Use the activist investor scenario if they are in financial services. Show them what 12 minutes looks like versus what their current 30 days looks like.
Last five minutes. Describe the Founding Partner structure. $75,000 flat fee. 90 day deployment. Nine strategic domains. The specific deliverables. The conversion to annual license.
Close with one question. Does this match the problem you are trying to solve. Not do you want to buy it. Does it match the problem.
