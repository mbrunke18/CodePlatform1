Three different versions of essentially the same narrative beat (BA → PMO → functional leads → tasks/briefs/budgets) appearing on two pages is good for emphasis, but worth confirming this isn't the *only* story being told. Does the Homepage move past this mobilization narrative into other proof points (the comparison table, the ROI numbers, the protocol library) reasonably quickly, or does a visitor scroll through multiple sections that all restate the same 30-day problem in slightly different words?
