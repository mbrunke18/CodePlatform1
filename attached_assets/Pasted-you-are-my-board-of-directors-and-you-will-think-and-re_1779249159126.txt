Love this prompt. If I put on a “composite board” lens from those leaders, here’s the assessment and roadmap.
Board-style product assessment (composite)

Perspective by “board member lens”
Bill Gates lens (platform + enterprise durability)
Biggest strength: systems thinking and orchestration layer potential.
Biggest concern: connector depth, enterprise integration, and data flywheel defensibility.
Recommendation: invest in interoperability and event telemetry architecture now.
Warren Buffett lens (moat + economics)
Biggest strength: clear pain with high cost of delay.
Biggest concern: can this become predictable recurring cash flow, not founder-dependent custom work?
Recommendation: simplify pricing and prove retention/expansion economics fast.
Steve Jobs lens (product experience + clarity)
Biggest strength: emotionally clear problem (30 days vs 12 minutes).
Biggest concern: any UX friction kills trust in high-stakes moments.
Recommendation: ruthless UX simplification; every critical flow should feel obvious in under 30 seconds.
Michelle Obama lens (trust + leadership + impact)
Biggest strength: human authority and responsible AI posture.
Biggest concern: trust narrative must be explicit and consistent across product/brand.
Recommendation: make ethics/governance visible; tell the human story behind the mission.
Venus Williams lens (performance + repetition + readiness)
Biggest strength: readiness framing is fundamentally “championship preparation.”
Biggest concern: are users practicing enough to perform under pressure?
Recommendation: build recurring drill/score cadence into customer operating rhythm.
Richard Branson lens (brand + bold distribution)
Biggest strength: category-creating story with strong founder voice.
Biggest concern: speed of awareness vs speed of competitor storytelling.
Recommendation: roadshow + partnerships + PR moments; be visibly first.
Product roadmap (what this board would approve)
0–90 days: “Trust + conversion engine”
Standardize the Founding Partner package and outcomes.
Eliminate UX friction in top 5 critical workflows.
Add visible governance artifacts: authorization logs, decision trace, accountability report.
Ship “starter packs” by segment (startup, mid-market, enterprise).
3–9 months: “Repeatability”
Build stronger integrations/connectors and event ingestion coverage.
Add practice/drill operating system with readiness score trendline.
Harden multi-tenant controls, admin, and procurement-readiness artifacts.
Publish 3–5 quantified case stories (even if early/design-partner style).
9–18 months: “Defensible scale”
Build data network effects (cross-signal intelligence improvements).
Expand protocol ecosystem and partner distribution.
Operationalize expansion playbook (land → expand across domains).
Move from founder-led selling to trained enterprise sales motion.
Company roadmap (operating priorities)
GTM: Roadshow-first, conversion-focused, intimate executive rooms.
Team: Hire for product reliability + enterprise delivery before broad sales headcount.
Brand: Own category language everywhere, consistently.
Finance: Track CAC, payback, retention, expansion from day one.
Governance: Board-ready KPI dashboard monthly.
