Where investors will push you
1) “Agentic” needs architectural proof

Right now it reads as:

Smart workflow engine

AI playbook generation

But investors will ask:

Are agents autonomous or scripted?

Do they negotiate decisions?

Can they escalate dynamically?

Do they learn thresholds over time?

You’ll want one slide that shows:

Agent roles, e.g.:

Detection agent

Risk scoring agent

Stakeholder routing agent

Without this, “agentic” sounds like a buzzword overlay.

2) Decision authority clarity

Your slide says:

“Decisions pre-authorized”

That’s powerful — but dangerous if vague.

Investors will ask:

What decisions can agents make?

Financial thresholds?

Legal exposure?

Customer communications?

If you don’t bound this, it triggers:

Governance concerns

Liability concerns

Trust barriers

Recommendation:

Add a spectrum:

Level	Agent role
Assist	Recommend actions
Coordinate	Route + assign
Execute	Trigger pre-approved steps
Decide	Within policy thresholds

This shows maturity vs. recklessness.
