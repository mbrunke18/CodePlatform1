## What is your company going to make? Please describe your product and what it does or will do.

ExecuteIQ brings NFL-grade execution methodology to enterprise.

**The insight:**

The NFL is a $20B+ industry. College football programs are worth hundreds of millions. Every game, 60-80 critical decisions in 3 hours—wins, revenue, rankings, recruiting, brand value all on the line.

These organizations execute at speed not because of talent, but because of preparation:

- Playbooks built before the season starts
- Decisions triggered by situational and data awareness (what the defense shows)
- Roles pre-assigned—everyone knows their job before the snap
- 40 seconds to read, call, align, execute. 60-80 times.

**The problem:**

Fortune 500 companies face the same stakes—M&A worth billions, crises that move markets, competitive threats that reshape industries. But they improvise every time.

72 hours getting meetings on calendars, figuring out who owns what, defining the work. After 72 hours—maybe there’s a plan. Execution hasn’t started.

The NFL would never operate that way. Neither should a $10B enterprise.

**The solution:**

ExecuteIQ brings that methodology to business:

- 166 pre-built playbooks across 9 domains (market entry, M&A, crisis, transformation, etc.)
- Roles, tasks, and ownership pre-defined—everyone knows their assignment before the moment
- Decisions triggered by situational awareness
- Execution begins in 12 minutes, not 72 hours

**What the MVP does today:**

- 166 playbooks built and ready
- One-click activation with roles, tasks, owners pre-defined
- Real-time coordination—teams execute in parallel, leadership sees progress

**What’s next:**

- AI signal detection (trigger playbooks from market/competitive/regulatory signals)
- Enterprise integrations (Slack, Jira, ServiceNow)

**The cost of the 72-hour gap:**

- Ransomware: $136K/hour delayed (IBM)
- M&A delay: $5-50M synergy erosion
- Crisis delay: reputational damage compounds hourly

ExecuteIQ at $250K/year vs. one incident costing $5-50M. Payback on first use.

## Who are your competitors? What do you understand about your business that they don’t?

**Adjacent tools (we don’t replace them—we sit above them):**

- **Project management** (Asana, Monday, Jira): Track tasks over weeks. Don’t pre-stage strategic responses or launch execution in minutes.
- **Business continuity** (Fusion, Castellan): Disaster recovery documentation. Don’t handle opportunities, M&A, or transformation.
- **Strategy platforms** (Workboard, Quantive): Track OKRs quarterly. Don’t activate response when something happens.
- **ServiceNow**: Workflow automation for IT. DNA is tickets, not strategic moments.

**What I understand that they don’t:**

1. **The methodology matters.** The NFL doesn’t run on Asana. They execute 60-80 plays because of preparation—playbooks, pre-assigned roles, decisions triggered by situational awareness. No enterprise tool brings that methodology to business. ExecuteIQ does.
1. **The problem is activation, not tracking.** Competitors monitor over quarters. I launch execution in minutes. Different problem.
1. **Pre-staging is everything.** The preparation happens before the moment—not during. That’s how football works. That’s how enterprises should work.
1. **The moat is the methodology.** ServiceNow can build features. They can’t encode 25 years of Fortune 500 execution experience combined with how billion-dollar football programs actually operate.
