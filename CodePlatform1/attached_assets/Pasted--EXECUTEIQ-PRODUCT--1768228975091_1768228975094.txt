CHANGE 4: ADD ANTICIPATION SCORING TO SIGNAL DETECTION (PRIORITY: HIGH)
================================================================================

SIGNAL DETECTION ENHANCEMENT:

When Signal detects a potential threat/opportunity, add scoring:

MAGNITUDE SCORE (1-10):
  - 1-3: Manageable, contained impact
  - 4-6: Significant, affects multiple domains
  - 7-10: Strategic, could reshape market/business

TIME HORIZON SCORE:
  - Immediate (0-24h): Must act now
  - Urgent (1-7 days): Week-level decision needed
  - Emerging (1-4 weeks): Early warning, shape response
  - Developing (1-3 months): Horizon signal for anticipation

STRATEGIC RELEVANCE SCORE (1-10):
  - Aligned with stated strategic priorities? (1-3 = low, 7-10 = high)
  - Does this threaten stated strategy?
  - Does this enable stated strategy?

ANTICIPATION VALUE SCORE:
  - Could we have seen this coming? (Yes/No)
  - If yes: "6 weeks early" = high anticipation value
  - Track over time: Is our anticipation window improving?

DATABASE SCHEMA:
Add to signals table:
  - magnitude_score (1-10)
  - time_horizon (text: immediate/urgent/emerging/developing)
  - strategic_relevance_score (1-10)
  - anticipation_value (boolean)
  - anticipation_window_days (calculated: days before threshold triggered)
  - could_have_anticipated (boolean)

DASHBOARD DISPLAY:
Signal card shows: "Market Entry Opportunity | Magnitude: 8 | Timeline: 2 weeks | Strategic Fit: 9/10 | Anticipation Window: 6 weeks"

QUARTERLY REPORTING:
- "X signals converted to playbook executions this quarter"
- "Anticipation window improved from 2 weeks to 6 weeks"
- "Y signals flagged as strategic opportunities vs. threats"

BACKEND LOGIC:
- Create scoring algorithm (can be simple initial version)
- Store historical scores to track improvement over time
- Auto-generate insights: "Your org is getting better at anticipation"
