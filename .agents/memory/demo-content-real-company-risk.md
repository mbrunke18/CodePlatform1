---
name: Real company names in fabricated demo scenarios
description: Legal/credibility risk pattern when marketing/demo content uses real, well-known company names portrayed taking specific fabricated actions or quotes
---

Marketing/demo scenario content (activist investor scenarios, M&A demos, war-room simulations, etc.) can drift into using real, identifiable company names (e.g. "Elliott Management", "Blackstone", "Vista Equity") as the antagonist/actor taking specific fabricated hostile actions or being quoted saying things they never said, against fictional target companies.

**Why:** This is a genuine legal and credibility risk, not just a style nit — it directly matches the "show me this actually happened" skepticism executives raise when reviewing a demo. Real, well-known firms should never be shown as the source of invented quotes/actions in demo/marketing copy, even in a "hypothetical scenario" framing.

**How to apply:** When auditing or writing demo/marketing scenario content, grep for well-known real company/fund/firm names across all demo data files, scenario builders, and marketing pages. Replace with clearly fictional but plausible-sounding names (e.g. "Redshire Capital", "Ferngate Capital", "Corran Equity Partners"). Verify replacements read grammatically correct in context (articles, quotes, possessives) after bulk find-replace.
