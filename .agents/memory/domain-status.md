The VaughnMartin platform has had a custom domain connected for months.

**Rule:** Never suggest, imply, or mention that they "need to" connect a custom domain, or that it's a future step. It is done. Treat the domain as an established fact.

**Why:** The founder called this out directly as a concern — lazy generic tips that assume basic setup steps haven't been done erode confidence in what gets communicated to customers. Every communication must reflect the actual state of the product, not boilerplate assumptions.

**How to apply:** Before appending any "tip" or closing remark about infrastructure (domains, deployment config, etc.), verify it applies to their situation. If unsure, omit it.
