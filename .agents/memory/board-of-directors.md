# Board of Directors — VaughnMartin / Readiness OS

The user has asked that this board be held in mind at all times during every engagement. This is not a validation board. It is a challenge board. Their role is to ask the hard questions, name the gaps, and tell what is true — not what is comfortable.

## The Board

- **Bill Gates** — pressure-tests every technical claim. Asks whether capabilities described are actually built and defensible.
- **Warren Buffett** — focuses on the moat and the business proof. Asks how many times the founder has directly asked for the business. Free product without structure is charity; structured validation is a partnership.
- **Sara Blakely** — sold Spanx out of her car. Asks whether the founder has gone directly to one person, shown them the product, and asked for the business. Not a podcast. Not a post. A direct ask.
- **Richard Branson** — believes in bold offers and letting the product prove itself. Asks whether there is one true believer yet.
- **Barack Obama** — asks whether the narrative is reaching people who need to hear it or just people who already agree. Narratives without results eventually lose the room.
- **Serena Williams** — asks whether the founder is mentally prepared to compete, not just prepare. You have done the preparation. Now compete.

## Board's Standing Assessment (as of June 22, 2026)

- **Real product** — confirmed. 180+ protocols, live infrastructure, sophisticated signal pipeline, ADVANCE loop with causal hypothesis classification. Not a prototype.
- **Not yet a viable business** — no paying customers. The product is a well-constructed hypothesis about a business. The market has not yet confirmed the problem is urgent enough for someone to write a check.
- **Current strategic focus (founder-stated):** Closing Founding Partners. Not a public fundraise. The investor page has been updated to reflect this — "The Ask" section (seed round terms, cap, ARR projections) has been removed and replaced with the Founding Partner Program framing.
- **The ask count (as of June 22, 2026):** 2 direct asks made. Both are channel partners (not direct enterprise end-customers). Both saw the value, both proactively requested scheduling, founder has sent proposed dates. Board status: hot pipeline — but board flags the channel partner distinction as a critical strategic question (see below).

## Channel Partner Pipeline — Board Flag (June 22, 2026)

Both current Founding Partner prospects are channel partners. The board's standing challenge on this:
- A channel partner sees the value but is not the end user. Their "yes" is a promise to find customers — not a deployment commitment.
- The Founding Partner Program was designed around an organization deploying Readiness OS in their own operations (their own triggers, their own protocols, their own executive authorization). Channel partners require a different structure.
- Key question for the next meetings: are they being asked to deploy it in their own org, or to resell/refer it to clients? The answer changes the entire structure of the agreement.

## Founding Partner Program — Board-Approved Structure

Two organizations. 90-day structured validation. No subscription fee during validation. In exchange for three specific commitments:

1. Defined onboarding with protocol deployment milestones in the first 30 days
2. Structured progress conversation at day 60
3. Conversion conversation at day 90 + a reference regardless of whether they convert

**This is not free. It is a structured commercial validation with deferred payment.**

Strongest current candidates: Hajo Siemers (pending demo link response) and one organization from Wayne Roye's network after their conversation.

## How to Apply This Frame

When the user brings a decision, a piece of copy, a strategy, or a product question — consider it through each board member's lens before responding. Do not default to validation. Name what is strong and name what the board would press on. That is what actually serves the user.
