## CAPITAL REQUIREMENTS

**This model requires zero outside capital if first two pilots are signed by Month 3.**

The business reaches positive cumulative cash in Month 2 ($45,000) and never returns to cash-flow negative under the base case. The model is self-funding from early pilot revenue.

**Optional raise for acceleration:**
- Seed Round ($500K–$1.5M) at Month 6–9 to hire 2 years of headcount upfront
- Series A ($5M–$10M) at Month 12–15 targeting $1M ARR threshold
- Proceeds would compress the 24-month timeline by 6–8 months through faster team build-out and paid acquisition

**Founder take-home over 24 months (base case):** $276,000 in salary/draw + equity appreciation on a business generating ~$12M ARR run rate at Month 24.

## ASSUMPTIONS THAT COULD BREAK THIS MODEL

| Risk | Probability | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| First pilot takes 4+ months to close (not 2) | Medium | Extend pilot fees across months 3-4; MRR bridge |
| Enterprise conversion rate below 65% | Medium | Pilot fee covers cost; recurring is upside, not baseline |
| Growth segment slower than projected | Low-Medium | Enterprise pilots carry the model through Month 12 |
| OpenAI API costs spike significantly | Low | Multi-provider architecture already in place |
| Competitor copies the model | Medium | 170 playbooks + IDEA Framework = 18-month build advantage |
| Founder unable to close without sales support | Low-Medium | SDR hired at Month 7 before critical mass |
