HOW IT WORKS
================================================================================

1. CONFIGURE: Select and customize playbooks for your organization
   - Assign stakeholders to roles
   - Define trigger conditions
   - Set escalation paths

2. MONITOR: AI continuously scans for strategic signals
   - Competitor intelligence
   - Regulatory changes
   - Market shifts
   - Internal system alerts

3. ACTIVATE: When trigger fires, playbook launches automatically
   - Stakeholders notified simultaneously
   - Tasks assigned instantly
   - Documents staged and ready
   - Budgets unlocked

4. EXECUTE: Coordinated response in 12 minutes
   - Real-time command center
   - Progress tracking
   - Decision support

5. LEARN: Platform improves with every execution
   - Outcome capture
   - AI-powered analysis
   - Playbook refinements
